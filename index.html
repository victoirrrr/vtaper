Projected outcome · 8 weeks

At 0.5–0.7 kg per week you'll shift 4–5 kg of fat, landing near 16–18% body fat. Expect a visibly tighter waist and upper abs showing in good light and when flexed.

A permanently visible six-pack sits at 11–13% — a 14–16 week job from 22.4%. This block gets most of the way with the muscle intact. Rushing deflates the frame, and abs on a deflated frame read worse than none.

Coaching guidance built around your stated constraints — post-cholecystectomy fat limit, reflux, low bone mass, no current back pain. Not medical advice. New or worsening back pain, reflux that won't settle, persistent bowel changes, or any concerning symptom: stop and see your doctor or physio.
