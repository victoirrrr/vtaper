Calorie logic

1,810 kcal is a starting prescription, not a known deficit. Real maintenance gets inferred from your 14-day weight trend. Hold it unchanged for the first two weeks.
Losing 0.4–0.7 kg/wkchange nothing
<0.3 kg/wk for 2 wks, adherence real−100–150 kcal or +2k steps
>0.8 kg/wk + performance dipping+100–150 kcal
Waist down, weight flatchange nothing
One lever at a time — never cut food and add steps in the same week, or you won't know which did it.

The per-meal cap is a tolerance tool, not a permanent medical rule. Most people don't require a low-fat diet indefinitely after gallbladder removal. Keep the cap while it's serving you — if you find larger fat loads cause no symptoms, discuss relaxing it with your doctor rather than with a plan file.

Gut protocol · an experiment, not a diagnosis

Oats and chia are a trial, not a proven fix. Run them 7–10 days. Keep them if bowel regularity genuinely improves; if nothing changes, the cause was something else.

Six weeks · honest expectation

At 0.4–0.6 kg per week you'll drop roughly 3–4 kg. From around 22% body fat that puts you near 18–19% — waist visibly tighter, shoulders more obvious against it, upper abs showing in good light.

A permanently visible six-pack sits nearer 11–13%. That's a longer job, and chasing it faster is how you end up smaller, flatter and exhausted rather than lean. Six weeks, reassess with real numbers, then decide whether to run another block.

Coaching guidance built around your stated constraints — post-cholecystectomy fat tolerance, reflux, back protection.
